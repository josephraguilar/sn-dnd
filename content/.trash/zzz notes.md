## Our Prince Prayer
Our Prince,  
Who art in the Abyss,  
Harrowed be thy name.  
Thy Labyrinth come,  
Thy will be done,  
On Oerth, as it is in shadow.  
  
Give us your horns upon our heads,  
And accept our bloodied offerings.  
As we trample those  
Who oppose against us,  
Or shepherd them to salvation—  
Delivered, their souls, with either.
